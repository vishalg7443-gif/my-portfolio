About Me

Hi, I’m Vishal. I’m interested in how technology, finance, and business can work together to solve real problems.

Hi, I’m Vishal. I’m interested in how technology, finance, and business can work together to solve real problems. I’m especially curious about artificial intelligence, fintech, investing, financial education, entrepreneurship, economics, and digital products.

I started learning about technology without having a traditional coding background. Instead of letting that stop me, I began using tools such as Flask, Python, HTML, CSS, GitHub, Codespaces, and AI coding assistants to turn ideas into working projects.

I want to continue developing both my technical and business skills. My goal is not only to understand how products work, but also to identify valuable problems, design useful solutions, and build things that people would genuinely use.
